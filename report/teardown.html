The verdict

Critical — confirmed three independent ways

All twelve disparity levels of the cost volume are bit-identical. The network performs no disparity search whatsoever.

The cost volume is built by shifting the left feature map by d columns and subtracting the right feature map, for d = 0…11. The shift is written as a zero-pad followed by a slice — but the slice takes columns [0:W] instead of [d:d+W], which returns the unpadded tensor unchanged. Every level therefore computes the same left − right at zero disparity.

What survives into the disparity axis is not matching cost. It is the zero-padding boundary of the 3D convolutions that filter the volume — a fixed, image-independent profile. The soft-argmin decodes that profile into a near-constant disparity prior, and the refinement head, which carries 90.6% of the model's compute, is left to recover depth from the left image alone.

This is not a quantization artifact and not a Hailo compilation defect. It is present in the float ONNX, it traces to a single line in the upstream reference implementation, and it is faithfully compiled into the shipped HEF.

Recovered architecture

Two 368×1232×3 inputs, one 368×1232×1 disparity output. The feature extractor is Siamese by weight but unrolled twice in the graph. Downsampling is four stride-2 5×5 convolutions, so matching happens at 1/16 resolution, and D = 192/16 = 12 gives the intended 192-pixel maximum disparity.

Defect register

CV-1

Cost volume carries no disparity information

Critical

The shift-by-d is a no-op: cat(origin, zeros_d)[:, :, :, :W] returns origin. All 12 levels reduce to left − right at zero disparity. The model cannot perform stereo matching at any disparity. Correct form is [:, :, :, d:d+W], or pad on the left of the right-image features instead.

This propagates into everything downstream: the filtered volume varies along D only through the 3D convolutions' zero-padding at the disparity boundary, giving an image-independent cost profile that the soft-argmin decodes to a roughly constant prior.

Evidence  upstream utils/cost_volume.py · ONNX nodes 71–103 · onnxruntime probe: max|diff| = 0.0 across all 11 levels · filtered-volume channel means form a fixed padding bowl, −0.78 … −5.56 … −0.48

DOC-1

Published parameter count overstated 1000×

Documentation

The public table lists 623.1 under a column headed Params (M). Hailo's own stereonet.yaml records parameters: 623.1K. The units were dropped in the doc generator: 623.1 thousand became 623.1 million.

Summing every convolution weight and bias over the unrolled graph reproduces 623,138 exactly, which pins the provenance of the number. The count of genuinely distinct learned parameters is lower still, at 423,586, because the Siamese tower is counted twice.

Evidence  HAILO8_stereo_depth_estimation.rst vs cfg/networks/stereonet.yaml · measured 623,138 unrolled / 423,586 unique

MEM-1

21.8 MB constant is a disparity index ramp

Major

A single initializer, /Tile_output_0, is a 1×12×368×1232 float32 tensor holding the value c in every element of channel c. It is 91.9% of the ONNX file and 5.44 M of the 5.91 M shipped initializer values — the materialised torch.arange(D).repeat(...) grid from the upstream soft-argmin.

Multiplying by this ramp and summing over channels is exactly a 1×1 convolution from 12 channels to 1 with fixed weights [0,1,…,11] and zero bias. Folding it costs nothing in accuracy and removes the constant entirely.

Evidence  initializer dump: 21.76 MB, 12 unique values, one constant per channel · ONNX nodes 131–132

HW-1

Refinement head does not fit the accelerator

Major

Six residual blocks at 368×1232×32 exceed what a Hailo context holds, so the compile script spatially defuses them. conv47 and conv48 are each split into 22 sub-convolutions, then rejoined through nested shape_splitter and concat chains.

The shipped HEF contains 460 distinct convolution layers generated from 53 in the ONNX — an 8.7× expansion, with 338 defuse fragments. This is the direct cause of the 10.7 FPS figure, and it is spent on the stage that is compensating for CV-1.

Evidence  alls/generic/stereonet.alls: defuse(conv47, 22, SPATIAL) · HEF string table: 460 conv names, 338 _sd*/_ws fragments

NUM-1

Softmax runs at full resolution, unscaled

Major

The 12-channel cost volume is bilinearly upsampled 16× before the softmax, so the softmax and the argmin run over 5,441,536 elements instead of 21,252 — a 256× increase, with no temperature or scaling term on the logits.

An unscaled softmax over int8 logits with a narrow calibrated range flattens toward uniform, which biases the decoded disparity toward the mean of the ramp. This is the most likely single contributor to the 8.22 → 10.3 EPE degradation on hardware, and the compile script applies no mixed-precision or per-layer quantization tuning anywhere to mitigate it.

Evidence  ONNX nodes 128–132 · stereonet.alls contains only defuse and normalization commands, no quantization directives

CV-2

Zero padding contaminates the disparity axis

Minor

Even with CV-1 fixed, padding the shifted features with zeros makes the cost at the image border 0 − right, a magnitude term rather than a matching term. On a 1232-pixel-wide image the first 11 cells at 1/16 resolution — 176 pixels, 14% of image width — would carry a systematic left-edge bias. Standard practice is to mask those columns out of the loss and out of the argmin.

Evidence  all 11 pad initializers verified all-zero · /Constant_output_0 … /Constant_50_output_0

EXP-1

Export loads weights non-strictly

Minor

Upstream export.py calls load_state_dict(…, strict=False), which silently accepts a checkpoint with missing or renamed keys and leaves those tensors at their random initialisation. Combined with CV-1 there is no assertion anywhere that the exported graph is the trained graph. Any rebuild should load strictly and assert a float parity check against the training model before export.

Evidence  upstream export.py · also exports at 224×224, not the 368×1232 the zoo publishes

DOC-2

Published hardware support contradicts the config

Documentation

The model's YAML declares supported_hw_arch: [hailo15h, hailo10h]. The Model Zoo nonetheless publishes a Hailo-8 page for it, with Hailo-8 FPS measurements and a compiled Hailo-8 HEF. One of the two is stale; plan the rebuild against a target you have confirmed rather than against this table.

Evidence  cfg/networks/stereonet.yaml vs docs/public_models/HAILO8/

ARCH-1

Single-stage refinement at 1/16 matching resolution

Minor

StereoNet's design intent is hierarchical: refine at 1/8, 1/4, 1/2 and 1/1, each stage correcting the one below. This implementation refines once, at full resolution, from a 1/16 disparity map. Each matching bin is worth 16 pixels of disparity, and the dilation ladder 1-2-4-8 gives roughly 31 pixels of receptive field to correct it. Even with matching repaired, the quantisation of the disparity axis will dominate the error.

Evidence  single Resize node (128) · upstream forward_stage3

What the rebuild has to change

Ordered by how much each one buys. The first is not optional — without it, nothing else in the model is doing stereo.

  1. Fix the shift, then assert it

    Slice [d:d+W], and add a unit test that feeds a synthetic pair at known disparity and checks the argmin lands in the right bin. A test that only checks output shape would have passed on the broken model for three years.

  2. Move soft-argmin below the upsample

    Decode disparity at 1/16, then upsample one channel instead of twelve. 256× less softmax, and it removes the full-resolution 12-channel tensor from the dataflow entirely. Add a learned temperature on the logits so int8 calibration has a range to work with.

  3. Fold the ramp into a 1×1 convolution

    Weights [0…11], no bias, exactly equivalent. Deletes 91.9% of the model file and one DDR-resident constant.

  4. Rebalance the budget away from full-resolution refinement

    90.6% of operations in a head that exists to compensate for a broken front end is the wrong shape. Refine hierarchically at 1/8 and 1/4 with a thin full-resolution stage, sized so no layer needs spatial defusing on the target device.

  5. Raise matching resolution, or interpolate the disparity axis

    Match at 1/8 with D=24, or keep 1/16 and add sub-bin interpolation around the argmin. Either recovers most of the 16-pixel quantisation ARCH-1 leaves on the table.

  6. Mask the invalid left border

    Exclude the first D columns from the loss and from evaluation, so the model is not trained to fit padding artifacts.
